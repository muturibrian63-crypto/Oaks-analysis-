Reload reads data/predictions.json from this site. Open a file to view a predictions.json from your own computer.

Estimates only. Probabilities come from form, Elo, three classifiers and a Poisson goal model, blended with bookmaker odds where they exist. The free data source has odds only for results and over/under 2.5, so every other market shows a fair price and waits for odds you type in. Edge is the model probability minus 1 divided by the odds, which keeps the bookmaker margin in. Odds, saved matches and settings stay in this browser. A value flag is a screening signal, not a recommendation to bet.
